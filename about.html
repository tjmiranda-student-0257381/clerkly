The short version

I started Clerkly because good help is hard to keep

I spent years watching small businesses cycle through virtual assistants. The pattern was always the same: someone capable arrives, spends a month learning the ropes, then leaves — and all that context walks out with them. The business is back where it started, only more tired.

Clerkly is built the opposite way. I take on a small number of clients so I can go deep on each one. I write down every recurring process as we go, in a document you own, so the knowledge stays in your business whatever happens next.

The other half of the story is technical. Alongside the admin work I build and maintain websites, wire up automations, and fix the small software problems that usually sit in a queue for months. That combination — an assistant who can also ship — is what most of my clients hire me for.

How I work

Four rules I don’t break

These aren’t marketing lines. They’re the operating agreement I hold myself to on every engagement.

Write it down

Anything done more than twice becomes a documented process in your own workspace. No knowledge hostages.

Say the hard thing

If a task is a waste of money, if a deadline is unrealistic, if I’m the wrong person — you hear it early, not after the invoice.

Guard the keys

Shared access through a password manager, 2FA on every account, least privilege by default, and a clean offboarding whenever we part ways.

Report honestly

A weekly summary of what shipped, what stalled and what I need from you. Including the weeks that didn’t go well.

Honest fit

Who I’m great for — and who I’m not

A good fit if…

  • You’re a founder or small team drowning in operational detail
  • You want one accountable person, not a ticket queue
  • You have work that spans admin and technical tasks
  • You value written updates over standing meetings
  • You want processes documented as they’re built

Probably not a fit if…

  • You need 24/7 or overnight coverage from one person
  • You’re looking for the cheapest hourly rate available
  • You need a team of five assistants starting Monday
  • The work requires an in-person presence
  • You need regulated advice — legal, tax or medical
