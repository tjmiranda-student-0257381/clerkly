5. Retainers & hours

A retainer reserves a defined block of hours in Clerkly’s schedule each calendar month. Retainer hours:

  • are not refundable and have no cash value;
  • expire on termination of the retainer.

Exceeding your hours. Clerkly will notify you as the monthly allocation approaches its limit. Additional hours are billed at your plan’s effective hourly rate and only after you approve them in writing.

Time recording. Time is recorded to the nearest quarter hour and reported to you at least weekly. Recorded time includes work performed on your behalf: task execution, necessary research, and client communication about the work.

Offboarding. On termination, and within 30 days, Clerkly will hand over your files, documentation and work product (subject to payment of outstanding invoices), remove its access from your systems, and confirm in writing that this has been done.
